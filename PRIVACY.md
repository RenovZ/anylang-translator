# Anylang Translator Privacy Policy

## Data Collection and Usage

Anylang Translator is a browser translation extension. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

### What data is collected

1. **Web page content**: When you use the translation feature, the extension reads the text content of the web page you are currently viewing. This is necessary to provide the translation service.

2. **Selected text**: When you use the instant lookup feature, the extension reads the text you have selected on the page.

3. **User settings**: Your preferences (such as target language, translation mode, API provider selection) are stored locally in your browser.

4. **API credentials**: If you choose to use a custom LLM provider, your API key is stored locally in your browser and is never transmitted to us.

### How data is used

- **Translation services**: Web page text and selected text are sent to the translation service you have chosen (e.g., Google Translate, Microsoft Translator, or a user-configured LLM API) solely for the purpose of obtaining translations.

- **Local storage**: All settings, API keys, and translation caches are stored locally on your device using the browser's built-in storage APIs. We do not have access to this data.

### What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information.
- We do **not** track your browsing history.
- We do **not** sell or share your data with any third parties.
- We do **not** run any analytics or telemetry.

### Third-party services

When you use the translation feature, your text is sent to external translation services based on your choice:

- **Google Translate**: Governed by Google's Privacy Policy
- **Microsoft Translator**: Governed by Microsoft's Privacy Policy
- **User-configured LLM providers** (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.): Governed by the respective provider's privacy policy

Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services before use.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
